🎯 1. Core Portfolio Health & Drift Metrics

Metrics measuring how closely your current real wealth aligns with your target financial plan.

Portfolio Alignment Score 0% to 100%

Measures the mathematical similarity between your current asset weights and your target asset allocation (e.g., Moderate 50/20/15/15).

How It Is Calculated Cosine vector similarity & normalized shortfall: Score = 100 - (0.5 × Sum of Absolute Category Drifts)
What The Numbers Mean • 90% – 100%: Optimal Alignment. Portfolio is humming smoothly.
• 75% – 89%: Moderate Drift. Minor rebalancing via fresh inflows recommended.
• < 75%: High Drift. Significant deviation from risk tolerance.
💡 How to Take Action Do not sell assets immediately (which triggers 12.5% LTCG tax and exit loads). Instead, use the Inflow Rebalance Simulator to route upcoming monthly SIPs and bonuses into underweighted buckets until the score reaches >90%.
Category Drift (Δ%) Over / Underweight

The percentage point difference between what you actually hold in an asset class vs. your targeted risk plan.

Example If your target for Equities is 50%, but a bull market pushed your equities to 62%, your drift is +12.0% (Overweight). If Gold is at 5% vs. 15% target, drift is -10.0% (Underweight).
💡 How to Take Action Negative drift indicates where your portfolio is defensively starved. Direct your fresh capital tranches toward negative drift categories to buy valuable assets at relatively lower valuations.
Cash Drag (₹ and %) Opportunity Cost

The invisible annual wealth penalty caused by keeping excess idle cash sitting in low-yield savings accounts (2.7%–3.5%) instead of productive compounding assets.

Formula Cash Drag = (Excess Cash above 6M Buffer) × (Expected Portfolio Return - 3.0%)
Real World Impact Having ₹10 Lakhs idle beyond emergency needs creates a drag of ₹70,000 to ₹90,000 every single year against retail inflation.
💡 How to Take Action Keep 6 to 12 months of living expenses in an Emergency Liquid Fund. Move the rest into Target Maturity Funds (TMFs) locking in 7.4% sovereign yields or Arbitrage Funds (yielding ~7.0% with equity tax status).
Emergency Runway (Months) Defense Metric

How many months your family can maintain their current lifestyle with zero income, relying strictly on immediate liquid cash and savings.

Formula Runway = Liquid Bank & Savings Balance ÷ Average Monthly Spends
Prudential Target • Salaried: 6 to 9 Months.
• Self-Employed / Business: 12 to 18 Months.
• Less than 3 Months: High Vulnerability.
💡 How to Take Action If your runway is below 6 months, halt all aggressive discretionary equity lump sums until your liquid buffer reaches safe ground.
🌐 2. Global Macro Weather & Market Regimes

Real-time indicators streaming via Google Finance that drive tactical inflow adjustments.

Composite Macro Regime Score 0 to 100 Gauge

A weighted composite health score evaluating global liquidity, interest rates, currency volatility, and domestic market momentum.

The 4 Macro Regimes • Risk-On (70–100): Low volatility, stable yields. Favorable for equities and SIP step-ups.
• Neutral (40–69): Balanced conditions. Follow standard strategic allocation.
• Risk-Off (<40): High stress, spiking volatility. Route fresh capital to Gold & TMFs.
• Stagflation Alert: High crude oil + elevated yields. Overweight commodities and short-duration debt.
💡 How to Take Action Check the Macro Weather Bar on the Home Screen before deploying lump sums. If in "Risk-Off", do not panic sell; simply route new monthly tranches into defensive yields.
US 10Y Treasury Yield (`TNX`) Global Hurdle Rate

The interest rate paid by the US Government on 10-year bonds. It serves as the baseline "risk-free" hurdle rate for global financial institutions.

Why It Affects Indian Portfolios When US yields rise above 4.30%–4.50%, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) pull dollars out of emerging markets (India) to earn guaranteed dollar yields, weakening the Rupee and creating domestic equity corrections.
💡 How to Take Action High US yields mean Indian equities face foreign selling pressure. Take advantage of FII dips by maintaining your automated domestic SIPs.
USD / INR Exchange Rate Currency Health

How many Indian Rupees are required to buy 1 US Dollar. Over the past 30 years, the INR has depreciated against the USD at an average rate of ~3.2% to 3.5% per year.

Portfolio Defense A 100% India-denominated portfolio is vulnerable to domestic currency depreciation. Holding Gold and International Feeder ETFs (e.g. Nasdaq 100 / S&P 500) provides a direct structural dollar hedge.
💡 How to Take Action Ensure 10%–15% of your portfolio is allocated to Gold and US index feeder funds to capture long-term currency alpha automatically.
India VIX (Volatility Index) Market Fear Gauge

Measures expected 30-day volatility in the Nifty 50 options market. Known globally as the "Fear Gauge".

Interpretation • VIX < 13: Extreme Complacency. Markets are calm; valuations may be stretched.
• VIX 13 – 18: Healthy Normal Range.
• VIX > 22: Extreme Fear & Panic. Historically represents the most profitable long-term buying opportunities for disciplined wealth builders.
💡 How to Take Action When VIX spikes above 20, do not stop SIPs. Deploy secondary tranches into diversified index funds while market sentiment is in despair.
Nifty 50 vs. 200 EMA Institutional Trend Line

The 200-day Exponential Moving Average (EMA) is the definitive boundary between a structural bull market and a structural bear market.

The Golden Rule • CMP > 200 EMA: Confirmed Uptrend. Fresh equity capital is approved at full multiplier (1.25x).
• CMP < 200 EMA: Structural Breakdown. The app activates defensive caution (0.50x equity multiplier) to prevent catching falling knives.
💡 How to Take Action When Nifty trades below its 200 EMA, Net Worth Manager automatically diverts discretionary lump sums into safe Arbitrage / Liquid funds until trend confirmation resumes.
Brent Crude Oil Inflation Trigger

Global oil benchmark. Since India imports over 85% of its crude requirements, oil prices directly influence India's inflation and fiscal deficit.

Thresholds • < $75/bbl: Favorable tailwind for Indian corporate margins and GDP.
• > $85/bbl: Imported inflation risk, RBI interest rate hikes, and margin compression in automotive, paints, and manufacturing.
💡 How to Take Action Elevated crude oil indicates inflation risks. Balance your holdings with Gold and sovereign fixed-income to protect purchasing power.
🧠 3. Machine Learning HRP & Downside Risk Stress Testing

Institutional risk models replacing outdated standard deviation with true black-swan downside protection.

ML Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP) Optimal Weights

Invented by Marcos López de Prado (2016), HRP uses machine learning tree clustering to group correlated Indian mutual funds and stocks together, avoiding the dangerous math errors of classical Markowitz models.

Why Markowitz Fails & HRP Wins Traditional mean-variance optimization inverts covariance matrices. When two assets are closely correlated (like two Indian large-cap mutual funds), Markowitz produces erratic, hyper-concentrated weights. HRP organizes assets hierarchically like a family tree and distributes risk equally.
💡 How to Take Action Select the 🧠 ML HRP preset in the Portfolio Rebalancer. It automatically establishes mathematical multi-asset risk parity tailored for Indian volatility.
CVaR 95% (Expected Shortfall) Tail Risk Stress Test

Answers the single most important question: "During the worst 5% of monthly market crises (such as 2008 Lehman collapse or March 2020 COVID shock), what is the expected loss in my portfolio?"

Benchmark Comparisons • Nifty 50 (Pure Equity): Expected 1-Mo Drawdown: -18.4%
• Diversified User Portfolio: Typically -8.5% to -11.5%
• All-Weather Defensive: Expected 1-Mo Drawdown: -5.8%
💡 How to Take Action The CVaR stress test converts percentages into actual Rupees (e.g. ≈ ₹3,80,000 drawdown). If this Rupee figure makes you lose sleep, increase your allocation to Sovereign TMFs and Gold until the projected loss is within your psychological comfort zone.
Diversification Ratio (DR) Institutional Multiplier

Measures how effectively non-correlated assets (Gold, Sovereign Debt, Real Estate) cancel out the downside volatility of your stocks.

Formula & Standards DR = Weighted Individual Volatilities ÷ Total Portfolio Volatility
• DR = 1.0x: Zero diversification (e.g. 100% Equity).
• DR > 1.4x: Institutional-grade risk mitigation.
💡 How to Take Action Aim for a DR of 1.35x to 1.60x. Every 0.1x increase in DR reduces emotional anxiety during market crashes without sacrificing long-term returns.
Dual Momentum Inflow Multiplier Capital Efficiency

Formulated by Gary Antonacci (2014) and Cliff Asness (AQR Capital). Rather than allocating fresh capital blindly, this algorithm dynamically weights tranches based on absolute and relative trend strength.

Adaptive Multipliers • 1.25x (Bullish Momentum): Trend confirmed above 200 EMA with positive macro score. Accelerates allocation into leading assets.
• 0.50x (Defensive Breakdown): Asset class in macro distress. Fresh capital is withheld and temporarily stored in liquid yields.
💡 How to Take Action Keep the "Tactical Macro Overlay" toggle ON in the Rebalancer. It automatically adjusts monthly capital deployment to optimize your entry prices.
💎 4. Modern Indian Investment Vehicles & Tax Alpha

Institutional instruments delivering superior tax-adjusted yields over traditional bank deposits.

Target Maturity Funds (TMFs) Fixed Income Alpha

Passive index debt funds holding Central Government G-Secs or AAA Public Sector bonds that mature in a defined target year (e.g., 2028, 2030, 2032).

Why TMFs Beat Traditional Bank FDs • Zero Credit Default Risk: 100% Sovereign or AAA Government backed.
• Locked YTM: Locks in peak multi-year yields (e.g. 7.30%–7.50%).
• Roll-Down Duration: As maturity approaches, interest-rate risk drops to zero.
• Liquidity: Redeemable anytime at prevailing NAV with no 1% penalty.
💡 How to Take Action Match your planned liabilities (e.g., child college fee in 2030) with an equivalent TMF maturity year to eliminate reinvestment risk completely.
REITs & InvITs Fractional Real Estate

SEBI-regulated trusts owning portfolios of commercial Grade-A office tech parks, retail shopping malls (Nexus Select), or power grids (PowerGrid InvIT).

Key Metrics to Track • Distribution Yield: Typically 6.8% to 8.2% p.a. distributed quarterly.
• LTV Ratio: Loan-to-value leverage ratio (typically conservative <35%).
• Sec 10(23FD) Tax Exemption: Capital repayment portions of distributions are partially tax-exempt.
💡 How to Take Action Replace illiquid, tenant-hassle physical real estate with liquid, publicly traded REIT units offering immediate diversification across hundreds of institutional corporate tenants.
SGB Secondary Market Arbitrage Guaranteed Alpha

Sovereign Gold Bonds traded on the stock exchange (NSE/BSE) by retail holders seeking early cash liquidity before their 8-year maturity.

The 4-Way Advantage 1. Traded Discount: Frequently available at a 2.0% to 4.5% discount to spot gold prices.
2. Zero GST: Saves the 3.0% GST incurred on physical gold.
3. 2.50% Coupon: Earns an annual cash interest paid semi-annually.
4. 100% Tax-Free Gains: Capital gains are completely exempt from tax when redeemed with RBI at maturity.
💡 How to Take Action Never buy physical gold coins or digital gold for financial investment. Purchase secondary market SGB series maturing in 2029–2032 directly through your demat account to capture immediate risk-free alpha.
Factor / Smart Beta ETFs Rule-Based Alpha

Systematic rule-based ETFs that select stocks based on proven quantitative factors (Momentum, Quality, Low Volatility) rather than pure market capitalization.

Top Indian Factor Indices • Nifty 200 Momentum 30 (`MOM30IETFS`): 18%–21% 5Y CAGR.
• Nifty Alpha Low Volatility 30 (`ALPV30IETFS`): High risk-adjusted returns.
• Low Expense Ratio (TER): Only 0.20% to 0.35% vs. 1.50%–2.00% charged by active mutual fund managers.
💡 How to Take Action Allocate a portion of your equity satellite portfolio to factor ETFs to generate systematic alpha without active fund manager style drift or high management fees.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Investor Playbooks
Why doesn't Net Worth Manager recommend selling stocks to rebalance?

Selling profitable equities triggers the new 12.5% Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax (above ₹1.25 Lakhs) or 20% Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG), along with brokerage, STT, and stamp duty. Furthermore, selling prematurely cuts your long-term compounders. Net Worth Manager employs Zero-Sale Smart Inflow Optimization, directing new savings (SIPs, bonuses, dividends) into underweighted categories, achieving alignment without triggering tax friction.

How often should I review my portfolio drift and macro weather?

Checking intraday price movements creates unnecessary emotional stress. For personal wealth building, reviewing the Macro Weather Bar once a week and conducting a Rebalancer review once a month (coinciding with your monthly salary or savings deployment) is optimal.

What is the difference between Strategic Baseline and Tactical Macro Overlay in the simulator?

Strategic Baseline distributes fresh capital strictly based on underweight percentages. Tactical Macro Overlay adds institutional intelligence: if an asset class is underweight but experiencing a macro breakdown (trading below its 200 EMA during a high-VIX panic regime), the engine slows down fresh deployment (0.5x multiplier) and parks safety tranches into risk-free yields until momentum confirms a structural bottom.

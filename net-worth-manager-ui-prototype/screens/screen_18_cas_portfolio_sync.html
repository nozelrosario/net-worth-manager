How to Export CAS from CAMS

Step-by-step guide for manual statement export

1
Open CAMS eCAS Portal

Visit the official CAMS Online CAS generation page:

camsonline.com/Investors/Statements/Consolidated-Account-Statement open_in_new
2
Select Statement Type: "Summary"

Choose Summary (Holdings only) rather than Detailed. This gives a fast, concise 1–3 page balance of all your schemes across both CAMS & KFintech without decades of transaction logs.

3
Enter Registered Email & PAN

Provide the primary email ID and PAN registered across your mutual fund folios.

lock_reset
Crucial: Password Setting on CAMS

When requesting manually, CAMS asks you to set a password to encrypt the PDF.

  • Recommended: Set your password as your PAN in UPPERCASE (e.g. ABCDE1234F). This matches the auto-unlock setting in Net Worth Manager.
  • If Custom Password: If you set a custom password, enter that same password in the app's PAN / Password field before syncing.
  • Automated Monthly CAS: For statements sent automatically every month, the password is always your PAN in uppercase.
5
Drop PDF into Google Drive or Upload

Once CAMS emails you the PDF (usually in 2–5 minutes), save it into your Google Drive folder Net Worth Manager/CAS Imports/, or drag & drop it directly into this modal!
